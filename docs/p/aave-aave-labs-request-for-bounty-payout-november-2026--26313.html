Aave Labs. Request for Bounty Payout - November 2026

Aave Labs asks for release of a 1,000 USD bounty for one valid bug bounty submission, plus a 100 USD Immunefi fee, 1,100 USD in total.

Aave Labs, acting as a service provider to the Aave DAO, requests payout for one valid submission received through the Aave Bug Bounty process, which it says has been validated. The requested transfers are 1,000 USD to the submitter and 100 USD to Immunefi, the fee equal to 10% of the bounty, for 1,100 USD total. The post follows the same payout path used in earlier requests, most recently October 2026, and recommends processing the payments as part of the next funding update. Aave Labs says it will coordinate with TokenLogic on inclusion. The November funding update posted the same day (TokenLogic) includes the same 1,000 USDC bounty and 100 USDC fee. No details of the vulnerability are given. The post is at discussion stage.
