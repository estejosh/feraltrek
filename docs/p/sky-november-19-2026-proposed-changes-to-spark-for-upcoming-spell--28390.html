[November 19, 2026] Proposed Changes to Spark for Upcoming Spell

Proposed changes for the Spark spell of November 19, 2026: seven changes across Ethereum, OP Mainnet, Unichain, Avalanche and X-Layer, including deprecating Spark Liquidity Layer on OP Mainnet, Unichain and Avalanche, and December grants of 865,000 USDS and 45,000 USDS.

Part 1 of 2 of the proposed changes to Spark for the upcoming spell dated November 19, 2026 lists seven changes, none dependent on another. On Ethereum: upgrade the ALM controller to MainnetController v1.11.0, which sends USDC over CCTP V2 instead of V1 on every route; claim accrued SparkLend reserves and route them to the Spark Liquidity Layer and the Spark Operations Multisig (recurring); and transfer the December 2026 grants of 865,000 USDS to the Spark Foundation and 45,000 USDS to the Spark Asset Foundation (recurring). On OP Mainnet and Unichain: deprecate the Spark Liquidity Layer by returning remaining USDS and sUSDS to Ethereum and setting all related rate limits to zero. On Avalanche: deprecate Spark Savings and the Liquidity Layer by setting the USDC deposit cap to zero and maximum rate to 0%, closing the CCTP route and retiring the governance bridge. On X-Layer: onboard the Spark-curated Morpho USDC vault to the spUSDC PAU through the ERC4626 facet. The post says items 1 to 4 and 6 each go through their own governance route. Open questions: details of each route are in the full post.

Flags: winding down three networks, treasury grant 910,000 USDS total, several independent changes
