Risk Stewards: Supply and Borrow Cap Increases on Aave V3 / 2026.11.15

LlamaRisk recommends raising supply or borrow caps on four Aave V3 reserves (wstETH, mUSD, AAVE, EURe) across Core, Monad, Arbitrum and Gnosis, to be applied through the Risk Steward process.

The post lists these recommended changes: Aave V3 Core wstETH supply cap 850,000 to 900,000 (reserve at 98.9% utilization, about 93.4% after the change); Aave V3 Monad mUSD supply cap 50,000,000 to 75,000,000 and borrow cap 40,000,000 to 50,000,000 (99.8% supply and 84.4% borrow utilization now); Aave V3 Arbitrum AAVE supply cap 10,400 to 20,000 (100.0% utilization now, about 52.0% after); Aave V3 Gnosis EURe borrow cap 10,600,000 to 12,000,000 (96.7% utilization now, about 85.4% after). For each reserve the post describes holder concentration and health factors of the largest suppliers or borrowers. For example, one vault holds about 99.8% of mUSD supplied on Monad, and the largest EURe borrower holds about 69% of that debt. LlamaRisk states it will implement the updates via the Risk Steward process and that it is a risk service provider funded in part by the Aave DAO. Open points: concentration of mUSD supply and EURe borrowing in single positions.
