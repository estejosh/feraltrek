Short note on proposal 620 (deprecated Polygon markets reserves withdrawal) cancellation

The Woof team cancelled Compound proposal 620 (Polygon deprecated-market reserves withdrawal) and put forward proposal 621 for Unichain instead.

The Woof team, with Gauntlet and security service providers, is running a series of proposals to withdraw reserves from deprecated Compound markets to the Compound Timelock, as part of their deprecation strategy and to monitor a healthy reserves-to-supply ratio. Earlier proposals in the series were 610 for Linea, 615 for Ronin, 616 for Scroll and 617 for Mantle. Proposal 620 for Polygon was cancelled because a sudden movement of around 200k liquidity from the targeted Polygon DEX made it appear likely to fail. After consulting Gauntlet and the providers, the team pushed proposal 621 for Unichain instead. The post says the Polygon proposal will be relaunched next week.
