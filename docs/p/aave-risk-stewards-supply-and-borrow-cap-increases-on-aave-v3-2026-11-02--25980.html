Risk Stewards: Supply and Borrow Cap Increases on Aave V3 / 2026.11.02

LlamaRisk recommends raising three Aave V3 caps: USDe supply on X Layer, USDT0 borrow on Monad, and WETH borrow on Optimism, via the Risk Steward process.

LlamaRisk recommends three cap changes based on its latest review of Aave V3 reserves. On Aave V3 X Layer, the USDe supply cap would rise from 25,000,000 to 50,000,000; the post reports 45.8% cap utilization as of 2026-11-02, with supply concentrated (largest supplier about 76%, top three about 98%). On Aave V3 Monad, the USDT0 borrow cap would rise from 57,900,000 to 63,000,000; the post reports 100.0% borrow cap utilization, with the largest borrower about 26% and the top three about 57%. On Aave V3 Optimism, the WETH borrow cap would rise from 6,280 to 7,750; the post reports 98.5% utilization, with the top twenty borrowers holding about 91% of debt, all using wstETH as collateral. Post-change utilization is given as about 22.9%, 91.9% and 79.8% respectively. The post says the changes will be implemented through the Risk Steward process and can be reviewed on the LlamaRisk Risk Stewards Dashboard. Disclosure in the post: LlamaRisk is a risk service provider funded in part by the Aave DAO and says it received no compensation from the reviewed protocols for this work.
