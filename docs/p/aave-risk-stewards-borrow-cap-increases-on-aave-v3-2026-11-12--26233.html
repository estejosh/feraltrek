Risk Stewards: Borrow Cap Increases on Aave V3 / 2026.11.12

LlamaRisk recommends raising the USDC borrow cap on Aave V3 Core from 2.75B to 2.95B, after the reserve reached 99.0% of its current cap.

LlamaRisk reports that USDC on the Aave V3 Core instance has reached 99.0% borrow cap utilization (2.72B of 2.75B). It recommends raising the cap to 2.95B, which would put utilization at about 92.3% after the change. The post notes reserve utilization is 95.6%, above the 94% optimal point, and that the proposed cap exceeds current supply of 2.85B, so further borrowing stays bounded by available liquidity of roughly 126M USDC. The top 20 USDC borrowers have health factors between 1.02 and 3.03 with a median of 1.75, and the largest borrower holds about 14% of outstanding debt. Collateral behind these positions is mainly ETH and BTC assets, plus two positions each using USDe and sUSDe. LlamaRisk says it will implement the change through the Risk Steward process and that results can be seen on its Risk Stewards Dashboard. It discloses that it is a DeFi risk service provider funded in part by the Aave DAO. Open questions: how the change affects utilization and rates if borrowing keeps growing.
