Risk Stewards: Supply and Borrow Cap Increases on Aave V3 / 2026.11.08

LlamaRisk recommends raising the USDC borrow cap on Aave V3 Core from 2.6B to 2.75B and the USDe supply cap from 550M to 750M, after both reserves reached near-full utilization.

A forum post from LlamaRisk dated 8 November 2026 recommends two parameter changes on Aave V3 Core. First, raise the USDC borrow cap from 2.6B to 2.75B. The post says USDC reached 99.0% borrow cap utilization (2.57B of 2.6B). The top 20 USDC borrowers have health factors between 1.02 and 3.07 with a median of 1.76, and the largest borrower accounts for about 14% of outstanding debt, with collateral mainly ETH and BTC assets plus USDe and sUSDe. After the change, borrow cap utilization would be about 93.6%, and the proposed cap corresponds to 96.6% reserve utilization at current supply, above the 94% optimal point. Second, raise the USDe supply cap from 550M to 750M. The post says USDe reached 100.0% supply cap utilization, with the top 20 suppliers holding about 99% of supply and 18 of 20 carrying debt at health factors between 1.02 and 2.30. Open questions: concentration among suppliers and reserve utilization above the optimal point.
