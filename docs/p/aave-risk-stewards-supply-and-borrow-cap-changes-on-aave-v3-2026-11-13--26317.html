Risk Stewards: Supply and Borrow Cap Changes on Aave V3 / 2026.11.13

LlamaRisk recommends one cap increase (cbBTC) and several cuts on Aave V3 Core and Mantle, including USDT0 supply cap 312.5M to 150M.

LlamaRisk recommends these changes from its latest review of Aave V3 reserves.
