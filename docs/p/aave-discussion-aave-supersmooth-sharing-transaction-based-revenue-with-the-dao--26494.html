[Discussion] Aave × Supersmooth: sharing transaction based revenue with the DAO

Supersmooth.fi asks for feedback on becoming Aave's official partner for position management and sharing a portion of its transaction fees with the DAO.

Supersmooth.fi proposes to become Aave's official partner for position management, covering Multiply, stop loss, trailing stop loss, auto sell and auto take profit, and to share its fees on Aave positions with the DAO. According to the post, the DAO's share would be split onchain at source and land in the Aave Collector in the same transaction the user pays, with no grant, retainer or build cost to the DAO. The post also mentions a free migration tool to bring positions from other lending protocols onto Aave V4. The author cites Aave interest revenue of about $134M annualized and V4 deposits of $1.51B and loans of $595.5M as of November 19, and frames transaction fees as a way to diversify revenue. The post asks for feedback from the community first. Open questions include the fee split and how the partnership would be presented to users.
