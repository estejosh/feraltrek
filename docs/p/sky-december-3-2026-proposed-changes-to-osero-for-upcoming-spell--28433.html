[December 3, 2026] Proposed changes to Osero for upcoming Spell

Proposed changes for the Dec 3, 2026 spells would let Osero send USDS to and subscribe USDS in a Test NFAT Facility for the NFAT Pilot, and raise Osero's allocator vault debt ceiling from 100M to 500M USDS.

Two changes go in the Osero spell: enabling the Transfer Asset Facet with a USDS transfer rate limit to the Test NFAT Facility ALM Proxy (max 1,000,000 USDS, slope 1,000,000 USDS/day), and onboarding the Test NFAT Facility through the NFAT Prime Facet with a subscribe rate limit of max 5,000,000 USDS and slope 1,000,000 USDS/day, to be raised in phases up to 50,000,000 USDS; withdraw and collect rate limits are unlimited. Two changes are requested from the Sky Core spell: registering the NFAT Prime Facet on the Sky PAU Beacon, and raising the DC-IAM parameters of ALLOCATOR-PRYSM-A (Osero's Allocator Vault): line from 100,000,000 to 500,000,000 USDS and gap from 15,000,000 to 150,000,000 USDS, ttl unchanged at 24 hours. Per the post, Osero is the Prime Agent in the NFAT Pilot, the first production test of NFAT Facilities; the facility is operated by Test Halo, a Sky Core Halo. The test draws up to 100,700,000 USDS, in single draws of up to 50,000,000 USDS, on top of Osero's existing debt. The post states the facet code matches audited commits.

Flags: debt ceiling raised 5x, new facility pilot, unlimited withdraw and collect rate limits
