LEGO Grant Proposal: RoundBeat for Lido community stakers

A LEGO grant request of $9,950 for 12 months to give small Lido CSM operators free access to a validator monitoring and alert service, plus two new views.

The applicant asks LEGO for $9,950 over 12 months to keep RoundBeat's public pages for both CSM modules free, give small CSM operators (up to 16 validators) full product access at no charge, and build two new views: 'on track for rewards' and a 'strike tracker'. The post says 428 operators with active validators and 24,041 validators were tracked on 25 November 2026, and that 303 operators (71%) would qualify as small. Budget: $6,150 for subsidised access, $2,000 for node data and hosting, $1,500 for the two new views and alerts, $300 for updates and a final report. Timeline: a set-up guide in weeks 1 to 2, the rewards view in weeks 3 to 8, the strike tracker in weeks 9 to 12, with monthly updates and a comparison against the oracle's result after each frame. The post states the code is not open source and that its frame figures are estimates from public chain data; the oracle's result decides rewards and strikes. Open questions: whether LEGO funds a closed-source tool, and how adoption will be measured.

Flags: grant request $9,950, closed-source code, figures are the applicant's own estimates
Status: Discussion, no vote yet.
