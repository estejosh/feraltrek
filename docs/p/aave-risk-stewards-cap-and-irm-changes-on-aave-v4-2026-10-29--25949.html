Risk Stewards: Cap and IRM Changes on Aave V4 / 2026.10.29

LlamaRisk recommends cap and interest-rate changes for Aave V4 on Ethereum, Avalanche and Base, including a USDC draw cap increase on the Core Hub Main Spoke from 36M to 60M.

LlamaRisk's Risk Steward post for Aave V4 (2026.10.29) recommends, on Ethereum: raise the USDC add cap on the Core Hub Main Spoke from 75M to 85M and the draw cap from 36M to 60M; raise USDC slope1 on the Core Hub from 5.00% to 5.50% and lower it on the Prime Hub from 5.00% to 4.50%; raise USDG add cap from 110M to 120M and draw cap from 75M to 80M; raise weETH add cap from 20K to 30K; and raise add and draw caps for USDC and USDT on the Plus Hub Ethena Ecosystem Spoke to 20M and 19M. On Avalanche: raise the WAVAX add cap from 2M to 4M and cut USDt draw caps on two spokes. On Base: raise the GOOGLc add cap on the Equities Hub from 15K to 25K. The post says the USDC draw cap on the Main Spoke is fully utilized (100%) and Core Hub utilization is 85.2% against a 92% optimal ratio. The slope1 change would move the USDC borrow rate from 4.63% to 5.09% at current utilization.
