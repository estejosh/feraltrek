[Direct-to-AIP] Ethena E-Mode Consolidation on Aave V3

TokenLogic proposes common reference e-modes for Ethena collateral (USDe, sUSDe) on five Aave V3 markets and retiring legacy e-modes whose Pendle tokens have matured.

TokenLogic proposes one set of reference e-modes for Ethena collateral on Aave V3 Core, Plasma, Mantle, Monad and Avalanche: 91% LTV and 93% LT for USDe against stablecoins, and 90% LTV and 92% LT for sUSDe against stablecoins. Legacy collateral on Core and Plasma would be retired with per-asset LTV-zero flags, keeping existing liquidation thresholds so existing health factors are unchanged, except Plasma #21 (LT returns from 93.1% to 93%) and Plasma #23 (moves from 90.1% / 92.1% to 90% / 92%). The proposal also retires matured PTs on Monad and X Layer #7 and #8, and removes USDe borrowing from Avalanche #4, where about $1.4k of USDe is borrowed. The post says legacy USDe e-modes on Core and Plasma hold approximately $709M of collateral and $637M of debt, and that parameters differ between modes. At the 10 November snapshot the post reports no liquidations from the changes; the lowest resulting health factor is 1.008. Borrowers wanting to keep borrowing USDC, USDT or USDT0 would move to the reference e-mode in one transaction; USDe and USDtb debt is not permitted there. Open questions: effect on users with thin health-factor margins.
