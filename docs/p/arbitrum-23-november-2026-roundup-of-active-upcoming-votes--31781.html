23 November, 2026 - Roundup of Active/Upcoming Votes

Arbitrum's roundup lists an on-chain Constitutional AIP in which Binance asks the DAO to release frozen ETH and ARB; voting ends December 4.

The roundup lists one on-chain proposal: a Constitutional AIP to approve release of the 8,425.997 ETH and 74,559,805.2 ARB currently immobilized on Arbitrum One. According to the post, Binance proposes the release. The funds were frozen by the Security Council on October 29 as part of a wider ecosystem effort to halt theft of funds following an exploit of Binance's hot wallet. If approved, they would go to Binance's recovery address to be applied to losses and costs from the incident, including replenishing the SAFU Fund and treasury reserves used to cover affected users. Because a DAO action is required, the proposal would perform an atomic on-chain action similar to the Security Council's: upgrade the Inbox contract on Ethereum to add a temporary function, send a transaction recovering the funds from the frozen address, then upgrade the Inbox contract again to remove the temporary function. The post says voting ends on December 4.

Flags: large asset release: 8,425.997 ETH and 74,559,805.2 ARB, Constitutional AIP, contract upgrade to Inbox on Ethereum
