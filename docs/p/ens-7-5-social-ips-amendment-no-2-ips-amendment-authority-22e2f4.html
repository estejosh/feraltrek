[7.5] [Social] IPS Amendment No. 2: IPS Amendment Authority

ENS voters decide whether the ENS Foundation Board, not a DAO vote, may amend the Endowment investment policy, replacing the Meta-Governance Working Group as oversight body.

Following the Next Era of ENS DAO proposal executed on 11 August 2026, the Foundation Board holds the Endowment Safe and the Endowment Manager relationship, while the Investment Policy Statement (IPS v2.0) still names MetaGov as oversight body and requires a Snapshot for every change. This proposal amends the IPS so the Board can adopt amendments at its reserved-matter threshold of four of five Directors, after a 7-day public forum comment with a written rationale, and disclose them within 14 days. The Board becomes responsible for adopting and amending the IPS, setting strategy and allocations, agreeing fees and terms with the manager, reviewing reports, and appointing or terminating the manager. What stays with the DAO: the Minimum Runway and any withdrawal that would breach it, Section 3 (the amendment power and the Board role), and the ability to reverse any Board amendment by Social Proposal. The Endowment Manager monthly reports continue on the forum.
