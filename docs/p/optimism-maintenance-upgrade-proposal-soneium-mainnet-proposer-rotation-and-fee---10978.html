Maintenance Upgrade Proposal: Soneium Mainnet Proposer Rotation and Fee Vault Config Update

A maintenance upgrade proposal would rotate the Soneium dispute game proposer to OP Enterprise and repoint two fee vaults to its cost recipient, as Soneium's operator moves from Sony to the Optimism Foundation.

Soneium Mainnet is moving its sequencer operations from Sony to the Optimism Foundation as chain servicer via OP Enterprise, with cutover targeted for 2026-11-19. Most of the migration needs no governance action. Two items use the L1 ProxyAdmin owner role and are submitted here. First, the proposer of the SUPER_PERMISSIONED dispute game (type 5) would change from Sony's key to the OP Enterprise proposer; the implementation, anchor state registry and zero init bond are preserved. Second, the L1FeeVault and OperatorFeeVault would pay the OP Enterprise L1 cost recipient instead of Sony's fee recipient Safe, switch their withdrawal network from L2 to L1, and get a 0.15 ETH minimum withdrawal (L1FeeVault from 5 ETH, OperatorFeeVault from 0). The L1FeeVault holds about 0.2 ETH. That is six deposit transactions in total, executed through two superchain-ops tasks (eth/085 and eth/087) signed by a nested 2-of-2 of the Foundation Upgrade Safe and the Security Council. The post says neither change affects protocol behaviour, dispute game mechanics, bridge safety or end users, and that the SequencerFeeVault and BaseFeeVault stay with Sony. Under the Operating Manual it goes straight to on-chain voting under optimistic approval, with a one-week veto period and a 20% veto threshold. Open questions: none stated in the post.
