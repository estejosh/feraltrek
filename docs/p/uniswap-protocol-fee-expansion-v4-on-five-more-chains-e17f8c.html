Protocol Fee Expansion: v4 on Five More Chains

A Uniswap Snapshot vote on turning on v4 protocol fees on Celo, Soneium, X Layer, World Chain and Zora, and lowering the target fee on two Arbitrum stablecoin pools.

This Snapshot extends v4 protocol fees from the eight chains where they are live to five more where v2 and v3 fees already run: Celo, Soneium, X Layer, World Chain and Zora. It uses the same upgraded V4FeeAdapter and initial configuration as the companion fee adapter vote, and it does not enable fees on third-party hooks. Fees route to the TokenJar on each chain; UNI burned there is bridged back to Ethereum and sent to a burn address, and the post says this infrastructure is already live. It also lowers the target LP fee on the two USDC/USDG StablePair pools on Arbitrum, from 0.5 bps to 0.07 bps on the tick-spacing-1 pool and from 0.08 bps to 0.07 bps on the tick-spacing-2 pool. The post text still contains an unfilled placeholder for the companion proposal number.
