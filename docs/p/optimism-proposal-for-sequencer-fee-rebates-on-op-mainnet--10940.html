Proposal for Sequencer Fee Rebates on OP Mainnet

The Optimism Foundation asks to rebate part of OP Mainnet sequencer fees to select partners, for activity those partners bring, paid in ETH fee revenue rather than OP.

The Optimism Foundation proposes authorizing it to use OP Mainnet sequencer and priority fee revenue to give rebates to select partners. The post says rebates would be paid from fee revenue in ETH, not from OP tokens, so they would not add to circulating OP. Stated constraints: rebates apply only to fees from activity a specific partner brings; a rebate may never exceed the fees that partner generates; rebates are selective and used only where overall deal terms are favorable to Optimism; and rebates are earned on milestones. The post argues partnerships grow OP Mainnet, citing ether.fi TVL of $347M on OP Mainnet and roughly 29% of crypto card settlement volume, and notes that new OP commitments fell by about a third year over year per the Year 4 budget update. It also says rebates fit with the buyback experiment, which directs up to 50% of net Superchain revenue to OP purchases. Open questions include how partners are chosen and how milestones are set. The excerpt reviewed does not give a total cap on rebates.
