[Direct-to-AIP] Reserve Factor Updates Across Aave V3 and V2 Markets

TokenLogic proposes raising Reserve Factors on 54 reserves across 12 Aave V3 instances and Aave V2 Ethereum, which the post says adds about $3.5M a year of DAO revenue and lifts the blended factor from 12.0% to 12.7%.

The Reserve Factor (RF) decides how borrower interest is split between suppliers and the DAO; it does not change the rate a borrower pays. TokenLogic proposes RF updates on 54 reserves, using balances and rates as of 9 November 2026. Changes are grouped by rationale. Reserves with no supply yield, such as BTC wrappers and staked-ETH assets on several networks, would move to 100%, for a stated combined +$173,209 a year. Volatile tokens (LINK, ARB, WPOL, GNO) would move to 50%, about +$24,255 a year. USDe on Plasma would move from 25% to 35%, about +$217,872 a year. Plasma USDT0 would move from 5% to 7.5%, in line with terms the post says were agreed with the Plasma team, and that line is only included in the AIP once those terms are final; Plasma GHO would move from 5% to 10%. Stablecoin RFs on V3 stay at or below 70% except DAI.e on Avalanche. WETH on Ethereum Core is excluded to coordinate with other Core collateral work. The post frames this as part of streamlining Aave V3 as low-adoption reserves wind down and V2 is frozen. Open questions: effects on suppliers on reserves where yield changes by a few basis points.
