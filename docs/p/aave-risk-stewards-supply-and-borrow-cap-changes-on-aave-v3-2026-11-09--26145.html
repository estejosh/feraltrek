Risk Stewards: Supply and Borrow Cap Changes on Aave V3 / 2026.11.09

LlamaRisk recommends lowering most supply and borrow caps across Aave V3 instances, and raising one: the USD₮0 borrow cap on Arbitrum from 45M to 52.4M.

LlamaRisk, a risk provider funded in part by the Aave DAO, lists cap changes for Aave V3 reserves across the Plasma, Arbitrum, Core, Prime, Monad, MegaETH and BNB instances, based on user behaviour, on-chain liquidity and position health. Supply caps fall for 14 reserve entries, for example USDT0 on Plasma from 950M to 820M, USD₮0 on Arbitrum from 120M to 58.3M, USDC on Prime from 25M to 10M and LBTC on Core from 2,690 to 2,400. Borrow caps fall for nine entries, for example USDG and RLUSD on Core from 24M to 9M and BTCB on BNB from 140 to 17. One borrow cap rises: USD₮0 on Arbitrum from 45M to 52.4M, where the post puts current borrow cap utilization at 85.1% and the top 20 borrowers' health factors between 1.07 and 3.66 (median 1.67). The post gives per-asset utilization and 30-day supply and borrow trends, and the cap after each change would sit at roughly 25% to 99.7% utilization (LBTC would be near 99.7%). The post says the changes will be implemented through the Risk Steward process and can be reviewed on the LlamaRisk Risk Stewards Dashboard. No treasury spend is described. Open questions: the post does not describe a vote or a veto window.
