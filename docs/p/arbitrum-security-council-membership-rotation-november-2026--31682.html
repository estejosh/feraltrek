Security Council Membership Rotation - November 2026

An announcement says Immunefi's representative on the Arbitrum Security Council, who left Immunefi, will keep serving in a personal capacity after 9 of 12 members voted to allow it.

On 1 November 2026, 9 of 12 Security Council members voted, in line with the ArbitrumDAO Constitution, to let the representative serve in their own personal capacity for the rest of the term.
