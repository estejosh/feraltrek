GIP 152 - Quarterly Investor/DAO Update 1

The Gnosis team shares its first quarterly update: the Gnosis App is becoming Circles-first, with an alpha pilot in Kenya and a rebrand to the Circles App planned.

This is a progress report rather than a spending request. The team says the Gnosis App is becoming Circles-first, aiming to give underserved communities their own money to exchange value. Since the DAO vote was finalised, it reports launching alpha products (a user app and a group admin app) with the first communities in Kenya. It reports about 13,200 Circles minted across 38 people and 2 communities, more than 50 trades (mostly services), and 30+ communities on a waitlist, and vendors are now being added to a marketplace. The existing Circles website became the technical site for developers, and a new public-facing site was launched. The team says it added a designer, a community coordinator in Kenya and a fractional finance operations team, and that the spinout is legally and operationally complete. Next steps stated: rebrand the Gnosis App as the Circles App, which moves away from being a neobank, with a V1 release expected in under about three weeks, and continued iteration with the Kenya pilot communities. All figures are self-reported.
