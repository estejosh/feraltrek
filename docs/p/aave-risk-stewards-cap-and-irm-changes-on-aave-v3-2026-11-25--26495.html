Risk Stewards: Cap and IRM Changes on Aave V3 / 2026.11.25

LlamaRisk recommends lower supply and borrow caps on several Aave V3 assets and interest rate model changes for USDC and USDT on V3 Core.

LlamaRisk recommends parameter changes after its latest review of Aave V3 reserves. On V3 Core: supply caps lowered for WBTC (50,000 to 41,000), cbBTC (40,000 to 32,000), weETH (1,600,000 to 1,500,000) and wstETH (1,300,000 to 1,150,000); borrow caps lowered for wstETH (15,000 to 10,000), WETH (2,150,000 to 2,050,000) and USDC (4.0B to 3.7B). For USDC, optimalUsageRatio goes from 94% to 91%, baseVariableBorrowRate from 0% to 1%, and variableRateSlope1 from 4.60% to 3.70%; for USDT, optimalUsageRatio goes from 94% to 92%, base rate from 0% to 1% and slope 1 from 4.50% to 3.50%. The post says the USDC variable borrow rate at the current 77.9% utilization would move from 3.81% to 4.17% APR. Further cap reductions are listed for Plasma, Arbitrum, Monad and X Layer, and cap increases for HINC and mGLOBAL on ETH Horizon.
