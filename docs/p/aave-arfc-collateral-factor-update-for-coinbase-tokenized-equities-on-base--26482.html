[ARFC] Collateral Factor Update for Coinbase Tokenized Equities on Base

An ARFC proposes lowering collateral factors for six of eight Coinbase tokenized equities on the Aave V4 Equities Hub on Base, citing higher stock volatility.

The ARFC would update collateral factors for Coinbase tokenized equities on the Aave V4 Equities Hub on Base under the existing methodology, using data from 2026-11-19. Six of eight reserves would drop: MSFTc 79% to 65%, AAPLc 78% to 65%, GOOGLc 76% to 65%, AMZNc 73% to 65%, NVDAc 70% to 65%, and SPCXc 55% to 53%. METAc and TSLAc stay at 65%. Maximum liquidation bonuses, liquidation fees, collateral risk, caps and the USDC rate curve are unchanged. The post says no position would be liquidated by the update. Its reasoning: the typical S&P 500 stock's recent volatility rose from 27.9% to 34.1% a year, and the collateral factor is capped at 65% and derived from a haircut sized for a once-in-ten-years off-hours fall, the maximum liquidation bonus and an interest allowance. The new values would take effect as new risk configurations.

Flags: collateral factor reductions, tokenized equities collateral, lower borrowing limits for existing users
