Maintenance Upgrade Proposal: OP Mainnet Proposer Rotation

OP Labs proposes rotating the proposer key on OP Mainnet's permissioned fallback dispute game to new OP Enterprise infrastructure; it passes unless vetoed during a one-week window.

OP Mainnet's operator keys are moving to OP Enterprise infrastructure, targeted for 2026-11-05. Most of the rotation (batcher and unsafe block signer) is handled by the Foundation Upgrade Safe without governance. As a Maintenance Upgrade it uses optimistic approval: a one-week veto period from on-chain submission on 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-03, with a 20% veto quorum.
