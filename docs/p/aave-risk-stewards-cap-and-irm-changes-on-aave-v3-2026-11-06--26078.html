Risk Stewards: Cap and IRM Changes on Aave V3 / 2026.11.06

LlamaRisk recommends a broad set of borrow and supply cap changes across Aave V3 instances, mostly reductions, plus a USDe base borrow rate increase to 7.00%. USDt on Avalanche had reached 99.9% borrow cap utilization.

A forum post from LlamaRisk, dated 6 November 2026, lists parameter changes for Aave V3 across Avalanche, Prime, X Layer, Core, Arbitrum, Plasma, Mantle and Monad. Increases include USDt borrow cap on Avalanche from 22.8M to 30M, USDC borrow cap on Avalanche from 64.6M to 70M, USDC supply cap on Core from 3.0B to 3.5B and borrow cap from 2.27B to 2.6B, and X Layer caps for USDe and another dollar asset. Reductions include WETH.e borrow cap on Avalanche from 13,500 to 7,000, DAI.e supply and borrow caps on Avalanche to 1, wstETH, DAI, EURC, USDS, XAUt, rETH, syrupUSDT and LINK caps on Core, and several supply caps on Plasma, Mantle and Monad. It also proposes raising the USDe base variable borrow rate to 7.00% on Core, Monad, X Layer, Mantle, Plasma and Avalanche. The post says USDt on Avalanche reached 99.9% borrow cap utilization and shows a borrow distribution chart. Open questions: the effect of cap cuts on current positions, and the size of the changes across so many reserves at once.

Flags: many cap changes in one post, near-full borrow cap utilization (USDt Avalanche), interest rate change for USDe
