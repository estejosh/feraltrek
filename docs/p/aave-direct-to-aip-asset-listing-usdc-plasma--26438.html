[Direct-to-AIP] Asset Listing - USDC Plasma

TokenLogic proposes listing Circle's native USDC on the Aave V3 Plasma instance, configured in line with USDT0, and adding it as a borrowable asset to eight eMode categories.

The proposal would onboard native USDC (issued by Circle on Plasma since August 28, 2026) to Aave V3 Plasma, which today lists USDT0 as its only fiat-backed stablecoin (about $603M supply against $552M borrows, roughly 92% utilisation). Parameters would mirror USDT0 and the recent USDC listing on X Layer: 75% LTV, 78% liquidation threshold, caps mirroring X Layer. USDC would be priced through the Chainlink USDC/USD feed wrapped in a CAPO stable adapter with the upward deviation capped at 4% ($1.04), identical to the USDT0 adapter cap. It would be added as borrowable to the eight current-generation eMode categories where USDT0 is borrowable; no collateral, LTV, LT or liquidation bonus values change in those categories. The post notes native USDC liquidity is new (about 4.5M minted) and DEX liquidity is nascent, so caps can be raised later via the Risk Steward. Final parameters remain subject to Aave's Risk Service Providers. The author discloses it is an Aave service provider and states no undisclosed material conflicts. Next steps: community feedback, risk provider input, then escalation to AIP vote.

Flags: new asset listing, thin initial liquidity, adds borrowable asset to 8 eModes, parameters pending risk provider review
