[Gauntlet] Deprecation of the Ethereum WBTC Comet

Gauntlet recommends winding down the Ethereum WBTC Comet by setting borrow collateral factors and the remaining supply caps to zero, citing minimal borrow demand.

Gauntlet recommends deprecating the WBTC Comet market on Ethereum mainnet. The proposal sets the borrow collateral factor to 0% for all collateral assets and sets the remaining non-zero supply caps (USDC and USDT, 10,000,000 each) to 0. Liquidation collateral factors, liquidation factors and the interest rate curve are unchanged. With every borrow collateral factor at 0%, no new borrow positions can be opened. The post gives market data as of October 29, 2026: 13.42 WBTC supplied by 37 suppliers with a non-zero balance, the largest holding about 76%; no outstanding borrows since October 6, 2026; total collateral supplied under $10 across all assets; peak supply of 102.8 WBTC in December 2025; peak borrows of 8.87 WBTC in August 2026; average utilization of 17.3% over the last 180 days and 6.1% since launch; net reserve growth since launch of about 0.0023 WBTC. Gauntlet's stated rationale is limited adoption and minimal revenue. It notes that earlier deprecations (Linea/Mantle, Polygon/Unichain) used a 50% reduction to avoid pushing borrowers toward liquidation, which is not needed here because there are no borrows. LBTC and pumpBTC supply caps were already zero since May 2026. Gauntlet says it will seek community feedback and, absent material concerns, move forward with an on-chain proposal on Monday while monitoring the market through the wind-down. Open question: how existing suppliers will exit during the wind-down.
