Should the gnosis DAO fund a Compensation Plan in GNO for Affected Users of Personal Data Leak?

A draft GIP asks GnosisDAO to fund up to 25,000 GNO so each verified user affected by a personal-data leak receives the equivalent of $500 in GNO.

A community author's draft GIP (status: Draft, type: Funding, 12 weeks) proposes a compensation plan for users whose personal details, including postal addresses, were in a leaked database tied to a GnosisPay leak. Each verified unique victim would receive $500 worth of GNO, priced by a 7-day time-weighted average rate on the day of execution. The budget cap is 25,000 GNO, stated as roughly 10,000 claimants at the estimated market price. Eligibility would require proof the user's details were in the leaked database and proof of active use of the affected service before the breach. Verification would be off-chain through a trusted third-party auditor or zero-knowledge proof system, with a 60-day claim window. The post names NOCA as verification partner and lists 0 GNO for administration. Unused GNO would return to the DAO treasury. Phases: setup and rate lock (weeks 1-2), registration window (weeks 3-10), batch distribution and sweep of leftovers (weeks 11-12). Open questions: how the leak and eligibility would be verified in practice, and whether the cap matches the number of claimants.

Flags: treasury spend up to 25000 GNO, draft stage, not yet a vote, off-chain verification by third party
