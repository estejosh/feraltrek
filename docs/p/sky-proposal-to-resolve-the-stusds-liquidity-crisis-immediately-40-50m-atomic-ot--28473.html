Proposal to Resolve the stUSDS Liquidity Crisis Immediately: $40–50M Atomic OTC Debt-for-SKY Buyback

A forum post proposes using $40-50M from Sky's surplus buffer or treasury for an atomic OTC buyback of SKY from stUSDS borrowers, with proceeds used to repay debt.

The author says stUSDS is at about 100% utilization with roughly $159M trapped and secondary-market trading at about a 15% discount. The proposal: allocate $40-50M from the Surplus Buffer or Treasury to an atomic OTC buyback auction open to LSEV2-SKY-A borrowers. Borrowers would sell SKY to the protocol at a fixed or TWAP-anchored price (the post gives about $0.038 to $0.042) and the USDS proceeds would go straight to repaying debt in the Staking Engine. The author claims this would drop utilization to about 68 to 74%, reopen withdrawals and let the protocol hold or burn the SKY acquired. The post cites about $95M in reserves. These are the author's claims and estimates and are not verified here. Open questions: pricing, eligibility, size relative to reserves, and governance route.
