Request for a clear stUSDS withdrawal and liquidity plan

A forum post from an stUSDS holder asks Sky governance for a public plan to restore withdrawals, which the post says have been blocked for over a week.

A forum post by an stUSDS holder says withdrawals have not been possible for over a week. It reports, as its own on-chain observations, that the stUSDS vault holds about 161.7M USDS in assets with essentially no free liquidity, almost all lent to LSEV2-SKY-A borrowers; that the only exit is a small window when a borrower repays, which bots take in the same block; that only about 280k USDS was withdrawn in two days; and that the LSEV2-SKY-A borrow rate was raised in steps from 29.74% to 49.74% since Nov 21 without meaningful repayment. The post says secondary exits on Curve and Morpho are illiquid. It asks for a public timeline and mechanism for restoring withdrawals (examples named: a debt ceiling cut, forced deleveraging, a PSM or Surplus Buffer backstop), confirmation of how bad debt would be handled and whether holders would take losses, a fair withdrawal queue or pro-rata distribution of repayments, disclosure of the largest borrower positions and repayment plans, and a regular update cadence. These figures and claims are the poster's and are unverified. No vote is scheduled.
