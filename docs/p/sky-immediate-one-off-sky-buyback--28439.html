Immediate one-off SKY buyback

The Core Council announces a one-off buyback of SKY: 750k USDS a day for 7 days (5.25M USDS total), with the SKY burned.

The Council acts under its authority to modify the Treasury Management allocation (A.2.3.1.5), exercised through a public post by the Core Facilitator, confirmed by Core GovOps and the Core Council Risk Advisor. Steps: the Risk Advisor posts a recommendation, the Core Facilitator posts agreement, the Operator Multisig executes, and execution is announced publicly. After 7 days the Smart Burn Engine parameters are reset through the same process. Open questions: whether the module bounds are in fact sufficient and how reset is confirmed.
