Final Proposal (Version 2): Comprehensive Reporting & Audit Standards, Strategic Communications, and Budget Optimization for EGG 2027

A Lido forum proposal asks Lido Labs and the Finance Workgroup to publish fully itemised financial reports and audits and to build budget cuts into the EGG 2027 plan, instead of paying outside contractors now.

The post follows an earlier discussion about funding an independent financial audit and replaces it with a mandate: no immediate treasury spend on external contractors. It asks that all upcoming financial reports and audits separate each sub-product (LidoEarn, Wisp, stVaults, LidoLend) with standalone profit and loss, quarterly aggregate compensation by function, and line-by-line budget versus actual reconciliation tied to KPIs. For the EGG 2027 budget it proposes a 10-15% reduction in Lido Labs' overall 2027 operating budget, 20-50% lower initial budgets for sub-products with restoration tied to break-even roadmaps, and automatic funding reductions for underperformers based on H1 metrics. It cites a current burn rate of about $40M. It also asks to move Lido Poolside sessions to a monthly format with community-driven agendas. Lido Labs and the Finance Workgroup are only encouraged to incorporate these measures. Open points: how binding the measures are and which metrics define underperformance.
