[Social] [7.3] Select OpenZeppelin to audit Governor Nexus

ENS is voting on hiring OpenZeppelin to audit Governor Nexus, its new governance contracts, before any deployment. The audit is paid from an existing working-group budget, with no new DAO funding.

Governor Nexus is a new governor for ENS, built on OpenZeppelin Governor v5.6.1 by blockful. It adds a proposal type registry, Standard, Optimistic and Bond rulesets, mutable voting and a late-vote extension. This social vote picks the auditor before any deployment proposal. A vote Against sends the working group back to the other audit firms it considered with the stewards. The audit price is not stated in the proposal.
