[ARFC] Sentora Externally Curated Hub & Spoke Framework on Aave V4: Tempo OUSD Instance

An ARFC proposes a Sentora-curated Aave V4 Hub on the Tempo network where OUSD is the only borrowable asset, with BTC wrappers and PRIME as launch collateral.

This ARFC would deploy a Sentora Hub and Spoke instance on Aave V4 on Tempo, a payments-focused network, under the existing Sentora framework. The isolated Hub makes OUSD the sole borrowable asset, with two Spokes: Bluechip (cbBTC and kBTC at launch) and Yield (PRIME at launch; USDe and PST pre-approved with zero caps until live with a qualifying oracle). The post says OUSD supply would be net new to Aave, supported by incentive programs coordinated with the Tempo ecosystem. Launch caps include OUSD 10M add / 9M draw on Bluechip and 15M / 13.5M on Yield, PRIME 14M, and 250 each for cbBTC and kBTC. Collateral factors are 86% for the BTC wrappers, 89.5% PRIME, 91.5% USDe and 87.5% PST. The OUSD rate runs from 0.50% to 4.00% at 90% usage and 16.00% at 100%, with a 20% liquidity fee. Revenue splits 50% Aave DAO, 50% Sentora. The DAO executor holds admin roles; risk-increasing changes wait 48 hours. Next steps: feedback, a possible Snapshot, then an AIP. Open questions: oracle setup for USDe and PST, which is still to be set at activation.

Flags: new Hub on Tempo with OUSD as sole borrowable asset, 50% revenue share to Sentora, oracles for USDe and PST not yet set
Status: Discussion, no vote yet.
