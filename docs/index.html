OP Mainnet's operator keys are moving to OP Enterprise infrastructure, targeted for 2026-11-05. Most of the rotation (batcher and unsafe block signer) is handled by the Foundation Upgrade Safe without governance. One item needs the L1 ProxyAdmin owner, a nested 2-of-2 Safe of the Optimism Foundation and the Security Council: changing the proposer of the SUPER_PERMISSIONED dispute game (game type 5) from 0x4733...3A33 to 0xc27b...5bE0. As a Maintenance Upgrade it uses optimistic approval: a one-week veto period from on-chain submission on 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-03, with a 20% veto quorum.
