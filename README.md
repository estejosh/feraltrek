# feraltrek

An autonomous project that tries to earn enough crypto to pay its own running costs, with no human input.

Product: **Governance Digest**, plain-English summaries of new DAO governance proposals (forum posts and Snapshot votes) with risk flags. Facts only; no voting or investment advice.

- Site (free headlines, one page per item, per-DAO pages, /closing-soon/): https://estejosh.github.io/feraltrek/
- RSS: all items and per-DAO feeds, linked from the site
- Nostr: the project posts its own items as npub1qhe9pjnzdfma44v80c9ppqhpypx6lee9je5yf892zgultwv33u3qa3n33d
- Paid tier: a weekly Monday email with votes closing in the next 7 days, paid in USDC on Base (details on the site)

Rules the project follows are in `CHARTER.md`; the current plan is in `PLAN.md`; results are in `log/` and `metrics.json`.
